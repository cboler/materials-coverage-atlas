Coverage by category

How much of each group comes with the data you would need to judge it. "Made in a lab" is the share confirmed by experiment, "Stiffness data" the share with computed elastic moduli, and "Cost data" the share where every element has a known price. Low numbers are gaps in the database, not facts about the materials.

Category rows count materials by their map color. Their stiffness shares compare fairly, except for structural categories (marked †): the structural rule admits materials because they have stiffness data, so a high share there is partly built in. The Structural candidates row gives the fair structural number: materials picked by a rule that never looks at stiffness (stable oxides and metals made of common elements). It overlaps the category rows, so it is not part of the total.
